If you want to delete your account and/or personal data associated with Brave explorer, please follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to yiwanyouxi68@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request – Brave explorer.
  3. Include:
    • Your Google Play account email (or the email used in the game)
    • Your in-game player ID / nickname (if available)
    • Device model and approximate registration date (optional, helps us locate the account)

What we delete

After verifying your request, we will delete or anonymize personal data linked to your account, including profile data and gameplay-related personal identifiers, unless retention is required by law.

Processing time

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days after verification. You will receive a confirmation email when the process is finished.
